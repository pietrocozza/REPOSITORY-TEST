# Sublocazione (subletting) of residential property in Italy, incl. sublocazione breve/turistica, rent to rent and comodato (as of 12 November 2026)

Scope note: Italy only. Status of law checked against the AdE guide updated April 2026 and the 2026 budget law (L. 30 dicembre 2025 n. 199). Where a point rests only on law-firm blogs or search snippets it is flagged "(secondary, unverified)". Several Cassazione numbers come from secondary sources and must be checked on italgiure/DeJure before being quoted in a final report.

## 1. Codice civile: art. 1594, 1595 c.c. and the difference between sublocazione and cessione del contratto

### Takeaway
Under the general Civil Code rule (art. 1594 c.c.) the tenant MAY sublet unless the contract forbids it, but may NOT assign (cedere) the lease without the landlord's consent. For residential property this default is reversed by the special rule in art. 2 L. 392/1978 (see section 2). Sublocazione creates a second, dependent contract (the sublocatore stays the tenant of the head lease); cessione substitutes the tenant and creates a direct relationship between assignee and landlord. Art. 1595 c.c. gives the landlord a direct action against the subtenant and makes termination/nullity of the head lease, and the judgment between landlord and tenant, effective against the subtenant.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 1594 c.c., c.1: "Il conduttore, salvo patto contrario, ha facoltà di sublocare la cosa locatagli, ma non può cedere il contratto senza il consenso del locatore." C.2: for movables, sublocazione must be authorised by the landlord or allowed by usage. — [Brocardi, art. 1594 c.c.](https://brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1594.html); same text in [FIMAA Milano / Avv. M. Figini slides, PDF p. 8](https://fimaamilano.it/public/filetiny/Formazione/figini-sublocazione_locazionediporzionediimmobile_altricontratti_dirittinov.2022.pdf)
- Art. 1595 c.c. (Rapporti tra il locatore e il subconduttore), full text: c.1 "Il locatore, senza pregiudizio dei suoi diritti verso il conduttore, ha azione diretta contro il subconduttore per esigere il prezzo della sublocazione, di cui questi sia ancora debitore al momento della domanda giudiziale, e per costringerlo ad adempiere tutte le altre obbligazioni derivanti dal contratto di sublocazione." c.2 "Il subconduttore non può opporgli pagamenti anticipati, salvo che siano stati fatti secondo gli usi locali." c.3 "Senza pregiudizio delle ragioni del subconduttore verso il sublocatore, la nullità o la risoluzione del contratto di locazione ha effetto anche nei confronti del subconduttore, e la sentenza pronunciata tra locatore e conduttore ha effetto anche contro di lui." — [FIMAA/Figini slides, PDF p. 9](https://fimaamilano.it/public/filetiny/Formazione/figini-sublocazione_locazionediporzionediimmobile_altricontratti_dirittinov.2022.pdf)
- The direct action of art. 1595 c.1 presupposes that the tenant is in debt toward the landlord, and is limited to what the subtenant still owes at the time of the claim. — [Massimario Cass. civ., Ed. Ad Maiora (search snippet)](https://www.edizioniadmaiora.it/web/binary/download_document?model=product.template.website.specifiche&filename=file_name&field=file&id=927)
- Sublocazione vs cessione: in sublocazione a further, derived contract arises that depends on the head lease; cessione requires the participation of cedente, cessionario and ceduto (landlord) and creates a direct relationship between cessionario and landlord — Cass. civ. n. 861/1989 (11 Feb 1989). — [Brocardi, art. 1594](https://brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1594.html)
- If the landlord has not released the cedente, he can act against the original tenant for defaults of the assignee or subtenant — Cass. civ. n. 24792/2008 (8 Oct 2008). — [Brocardi, art. 1594](https://brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1594.html)
- A clause banning subletting is not a "clausola vessatoria" requiring specific written approval under art. 1341 c.2 c.c. — Cass. civ. n. 337/1979. — [Brocardi, art. 1594](https://brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1594.html)
- Effects on subtenant: Cass. civ. Sez. VI-3, ord. n. 9899 of 28/03/2022 (eviction judgment against tenant is enforceable also against a subtenant who was not a party); Cass. civ. Sez. III, ord. n. 14505 of 23/05/2024 (sublet becomes ineffective when the head tenant's title ends); Cass. civ. Sez. III n. 2675 of 11/03/1998 (an assignment/sublet not communicated cannot be set up against the landlord) (secondary, unverified). — [Studio Moscarini, 27/11/2025](https://www.studiomoscarini.it/2025/11/27/azioni-legali-contro-sublocazione-abusiva/)
- Cass. Sez. III ord. n. 17694 of 30/06/2025: termination of an immobile leasing validly notified to the user ends the sublease too; the sub-user must vacate (art. 1595 c.3 applied regardless of the agreed sublease duration) (secondary). — [Simpliciter, Cass. 17694/2025](https://simpliciter.ai/app/search/public/giurisprudenza/112be293-1094-5e0c-aa06-b5171bd3af36/cassazione/); [Brocardi art. 1595](https://www.brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1595.html)

### Inferences
- For a rent-to-rent operator the key structural risk is art. 1595 c.3: any termination of the head lease (for arrears, unauthorised sublet, etc.) knocks out all sub-contracts, including future short-stay bookings; the guest/subtenant only has damages claims against the sublocatore.
- Art. 1596 ss. c.c. (end of lease, rinnovazione tacita art. 1597, etc.) apply to the sublease as to any lease; no special sublease rule beyond 1594–1595 was found.

### Gaps
- Full text of art. 1596 ss. not reviewed in this session (out of core scope).

## 2. L. 392/1978 art. 2 (residential) and art. 36 (commercial): still in force? Clausola di divieto, risoluzione, art. 1455/1456 c.c.

### Takeaway
Art. 2 L. 392/1978 was NOT among the articles of L. 392/78 repealed by art. 14 c.4 L. 431/1998, so it still governs residential leases: total sublet and assignment require the landlord's consent; partial sublet is allowed unless forbidden by contract, subject to prior raccomandata to the landlord naming the subtenant, duration and rooms. For non-residential leases art. 36 allows sublet/assignment without consent only together with the business (azienda), with raccomandata A/R and landlord's opposition for "gravi motivi" within 30 days. Breach of a contractual sublet ban is an inadempimento, but without a clausola risolutiva espressa (art. 1456) termination requires a non-trivial breach (art. 1455) assessed case by case.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 2 L. 392/1978 (Disciplina della sublocazione), verbatim: "Il conduttore non puo' sublocare totalmente l'immobile, ne' puo' cedere ad altri il contratto senza il consenso del locatore. Salvo patto contrario il conduttore ha la facolta' di sublocare parzialmente l'immobile, previa comunicazione al locatore con lettera raccomandata che indichi la persona del subconduttore, la durata del contratto ed i vani sublocati." — [L. 392/1978 text, PDF p. 1](https://www.geometricagliari.it/wp-content/uploads/NORMATIVA_CATASTALE/1978/1978.07.27_-_Legge_n.392.pdf)
- Art. 14 c.4 L. 431/1998 repealed arts. 1, 3, 12, 13, 14–26, 54, 60–66, 75–79 "limitatamente alle locazioni abitative", and 83 of L. 392/1978 — art. 2 is not in the list. — [L. 431/1998 text, PDF p. 18](https://www.comune.sassari.it/.galleries/doc-testi_amm_trasp/legge_431_98.pdf)
- Brocardi commentary: art. 2, "diversamente dall'articolo precedente non è stata abrogata dalla L. 431/1998", remains the reference for residential sublets; Studio Cataldi lists arts. 2, 4, 5 L. 392/78 among provisions still applicable to residential leases. — [Brocardi, art. 2 L. 392/78](https://www.brocardi.it/legge-equo-canone/titolo-i/capo-i/art2.html); [Studio Cataldi](https://www.studiocataldi.it/articoli/13234-il-contratto-di-locazione-ad-uso-abitativo.asp)
- Partial sublet duration cannot exceed the head lease; sublease rent is completely free (not capped). — [FIMAA/Figini slides, PDF pp. 11–12](https://fimaamilano.it/public/filetiny/Formazione/figini-sublocazione_locazionediporzionediimmobile_altricontratti_dirittinov.2022.pdf)
- Art. 36 L. 392/1978 (non-residential): "Il conduttore puo' sublocare l'immobile o cedere il contratto di locazione anche senza il consenso del locatore, purche' venga insieme ceduta o locata l'azienda, dandone comunicazione al locatore mediante lettera raccomandata con avviso di ricevimento. Il locatore puo' opporsi, per gravi motivi, entro trenta giorni..." If not released, the landlord can act against the cedente. — [L. 392/1978 text, PDF p. 15](https://www.geometricagliari.it/wp-content/uploads/NORMATIVA_CATASTALE/1978/1978.07.27_-_Legge_n.392.pdf)
- Cass. n. 14454/2006: for non-residential, failure to communicate the assignment makes it unenforceable against the landlord, but the landlord cannot treat the tenant as automatically in breach; he can only oppose for gravi motivi. — [Brocardi art. 1594](https://brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1594.html)
- Breach of contractual sublet ban → termination only if not of "scarsa importanza" (art. 1455), assessed in concreto: Cass. n. 16111/2010 (8 Jul 2010), Cass. n. 17348/2011 (18 Aug 2011), Cass. n. 15763/2000. — [Brocardi art. 1594](https://brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-vi/sezione-i/art1594.html). Note: one secondary summary cites this as "n. 16611" — likely a typo for 16111/2010 ([Studio Legale MP](https://www.studiolegalemp.info/blog/affitti-brevi-in-condominio-la-cassazione-fissa-i-limiti.html)).
- Cass. Sez. III n. 9931 of 18/06/2012: mere hospitality, even prolonged, does not breach a sublet/comodato ban; giving third parties autonomous use does, and with a clausola risolutiva espressa termination operates upon the landlord's declaration of intent to rely on it (art. 1456 c.2). — [CondominioWeb](https://www.condominioweb.com/conduttore-non-viola-divieto-sublocazione-comodato-ospitalita-prolungata.25706); [Studio Moscarini](https://www.studiomoscarini.it/2025/11/27/azioni-legali-contro-sublocazione-abusiva/)
- Cass. Sez. III n. 14343 of 19/06/2009: a clause banning hospitality of non-family members is null (art. 2 Cost.) (secondary). — [Studio Moscarini](https://www.studiomoscarini.it/2025/11/27/azioni-legali-contro-sublocazione-abusiva/)
- Cass. Sez. III ord. n. 27955 of 07/12/2020: tenant's cure after the termination claim does not erase the breach (secondary). — [Studio Moscarini](https://www.studiomoscarini.it/2025/11/27/azioni-legali-contro-sublocazione-abusiva/)
- Cass. ord. n. 25875/2025 (22/09/2025): in the case examined, the landlord's refusal of consent under a "no sublet without consent" clause was not abusive (partial extract only). — [Diritto Pratico search](https://apps.dirittopratico.it/sentenze.html?q=sublocazione+recesso+locatore)
- Other remedies listed for abusive sublet: diffida ad adempiere (art. 1454), indemnity after termination until return (art. 1591, plus maggior danno), right of inspection (art. 1587), rito locatizio art. 447-bis c.p.c. — [Studio Moscarini](https://www.studiomoscarini.it/2025/11/27/azioni-legali-contro-sublocazione-abusiva/)

### Inferences
- Practical drafting: to make an unauthorised Airbnb sublet an automatic ground for termination, the head lease needs an express 1456 clause specifically naming sublocazione (totale/parziale), comodato and locazione turistica/breve; otherwise the judge weighs gravity (1455). Total sublet without consent is already prohibited ex lege by art. 2 L. 392/78, which arguably makes it a "typical" serious breach, but courts still apply 1455 absent a 1456 clause (Cass. 17348/2011 frames it as no "valutazione legale tipica").
- Partial short-term sublet of a room (e.g., Airbnb "stanza privata") is lawful by default under art. 2 c.2 L. 392/78 if the contract has no ban and the raccomandata is sent — but each guest change would in theory require a new communication naming the subtenant; no source addresses how this works for nightly guests (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No source found on whether a single "omnibus" communication suffices for repeated short-stay partial sublets of a room under art. 2 c.2 L. 392/78.
- Ord. 25875/2025 and 14505/2024 not read in full text.

## 3. Sublocazione breve and comodato breve: art. 4 DL 50/2017, cedolare secca, Circolare 24/E/2017, April 2026 AdE guide

### Takeaway
Yes — since 1 June 2017 a sublocatore (and a comodatario who rents out) can opt for cedolare secca on short lets (≤30 days) made as a private individual outside business activity; the income stays "reddito diverso" (art. 67 c.1 lett. h TUIR) and is taxed on 100% of the gross amount, no costs (including the rent paid to the owner) deductible. Before DL 50/2017 (circ. 26/E/2011) sublets were excluded from cedolare; this exclusion still applies to sublets >30 days. Rates as of 2026: 26%, reduced to 21% for one unit chosen in the return (L. 213/2023); from 2026 the regime applies only if no more than TWO apartments per year are used for short lets (L. 199/2025 art. 1 c.17); beyond that, business activity is presumed "da chiunque esercitata".

### Cited Findings
- Art. 4 c.1 DL 50/2017 definition: contracts "di locazione di immobili ad uso abitativo di durata non superiore a 30 giorni, ivi inclusi quelli che prevedono la prestazione dei servizi di fornitura di biancheria e di pulizia dei locali, stipulati da persone fisiche, al di fuori dell'esercizio di attività d'impresa, direttamente o tramite [intermediari/portali]". C.3 extends the cedolare (c.2) "ai corrispettivi lordi derivanti dai contratti di sublocazione e dai contratti a titolo oneroso conclusi dal comodatario aventi ad oggetto il godimento dell'immobile a favore di terzi". — [Confedilizia, Nota locazioni brevi 2017, PDF p. 1](https://www.confedilizia.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/Nota-locazioni-brevi.pdf)
- Circ. 24/E del 12/10/2017, §1 (PDF p. 5–6): sublocazione and comodatario contracts of the same duration are "equiparati"; locatore includes "proprietario o titolare di altro diritto reale, sublocatore, comodatario"; scope is determined by the "causa del contratto e non in base al diritto che ha sull'immobile colui che lo mette a disposizione"; excluded: lets within business activity (art. 55 c.2 TUIR, art. 4 DPR 633/72), including occasional commercial activity (art. 67 c.1 lett. i TUIR), and contracts where the tenant acts in business (e.g. foresteria); covers cat. A1–A11 except A10 plus pertinenze and single rooms. — [Circolare 24/E/2017 (copy ANCE Ragusa)](https://ragusa.ance.it/wp-content/uploads/archivet/30233-Circolare%2024-E%20del%2012%20ottobre%202017.pdf)
- Circ. 24/E/2017 §3.1 (PDF p. 18): "Per quanto riguarda la sublocazione, l'applicazione della cedolare secca non modifica la qualificazione reddituale dei proventi derivanti da tale contratto." For comodato: the comodante remains holder of the reddito fondiario; the comodatario/locatore becomes holder of a "reddito diverso assimilabile alla sublocazione"; for short lets, earlier practice (Ris. 381/E and 394/E/2008, which attributed everything to the comodante) is "superati". — [Circolare 24/E/2017](https://ragusa.ance.it/wp-content/uploads/archivet/30233-Circolare%2024-E%20del%2012%20ottobre%202017.pdf)
- Circ. 24/E/2017 §3.2 (PDF p. 18–19): sublet/comodato do not transfer reddito fondiario, which remains with the owner; declaration obligations of the parties unchanged. — [Circolare 24/E/2017](https://ragusa.ance.it/wp-content/uploads/archivet/30233-Circolare%2024-E%20del%2012%20ottobre%202017.pdf)
- Before DL 50/2017, circ. 26/E/2011 excluded cedolare for sublocazioni because the income is "redditi diversi di cui all'art. 67, comma 1, lett. h) del Tuir"; with cedolare the sublocatore is taxed on 100% of the gross amount without cost deduction. — [Fiscal Focus, 28/02/2018](https://www.fiscal-focus.it/quotidiano/altre-tematiche/notizie-free/modello-redditi-locazione-di-breve-durata-cedolare-secca-e-sublocazioni,3,95637); [EC News, "Nella sublocazione breve in cedolare secca i costi non si deducono"](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=22737)
- AdE guide April 2026 (PDF p. 3): "A partire dal 2026, la legge n. 199/2025 (articolo 1, comma 17 - legge di bilancio 2026) ha stabilito che il regime fiscale delle locazioni brevi può essere riconosciuto solo in caso di destinazione a questa finalità di non più di due appartamenti per ciascun periodo d'imposta. Superata questa soglia, l'attività di locazione breve, da chiunque esercitata, si presume svolta in forma imprenditoriale." Until 31/12/2025 the limit was four apartments. — [AdE, Locazioni brevi: disciplina fiscale e regole per gli intermediari, aprile 2026](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/locazioni_brevi_disciplina_fiscale_e_regole_per_intermediari_aprile-2026)
- AdE guide April 2026 (PDF p. 6): since 1/1/2024 (L. 213/2023) rate 26%, "ridotta al 21% per i redditi riferiti ai contratti di locazione breve stipulati per una sola unità immobiliare per ciascun periodo d'imposta, a scelta del contribuente", chosen in the tax return; "il proprietario dell'immobile (o sublocatore o comodatario) può scegliere" the cedolare; for sublet and comodato contracts the substitute tax applies "ai corrispettivi lordi". — [AdE guide April 2026](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/locazioni_brevi_disciplina_fiscale_e_regole_per_intermediari_aprile-2026)
- AdE guide April 2026 (PDF p. 8): "il sublocatore o il comodatario è tenuto a dichiarare tra i redditi diversi i ricavi derivanti dal contratto di locazione applicando, in caso di opzione, il regime della cedolare secca"; the owner keeps declaring the reddito fondiario (rendita catastale). — [AdE guide April 2026](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/locazioni_brevi_disciplina_fiscale_e_regole_per_intermediari_aprile-2026)
- Commentators read the 2026 rules as: 1st unit 21%, 2nd unit 26%, from the 3rd unit presumption of business and no cedolare. — [Il Sole 24 Ore NT+ Fisco, 28/05/2026](https://ntplusfisco.ilsole24ore.com/art/affitti-brevi-cedolare-26percento-e-attivita-d-impresa-ora-tre-alloggi-come-gestire-partita-iva-AIFRCRcB); [Diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/locazioni-brevi-novita-legge-bilancio-2026-consulta/). Diritto.it describes it as an absolute (iuris et de iure) presumption.
- Counting for sublet/comodato: commentary indicates the unit-count falls on the person who concludes the short lets (the sublocatore/comodatario), not the owner (secondary). — [Finanza e Fisco / Profund / centrofiscale summaries (search snippets)](https://profund.it/blog/affitto-breve-sublocazione-comodato-tasse-2026); [centrofiscale](https://centrofiscale.com/cedolare-secca-26-sublocazione-comodato/)
- Without the option, where an intermediary collects payments the 21% withholding is on account, not final; 2017 commentary flagged uncertainty on who is taxed and on comodato attribution (later resolved by circ. 24/E). — [EC News, L. Pietrobon, 9/6/2017](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/?p=110410)
- Comodato: a 2022 AdE answer (risposta n. 379/2022) is cited by commentators on a comodatario who uses the property as main home and lets it short-term for limited periods (not verified). — [search snippet via finanzaefisco/ecnews](https://www.ecnews.it/fiscale/fisco-e-patrimonio/imposte-sul-reddito/comodatario-cedolare-secca-nelle-locazioni-brevi/)

### Inferences
- A sublocatore paying e.g. €1,200/month to the owner and grossing €2,500/month on Airbnb pays cedolare on €2,500 (not on the €1,300 margin): cedolare is usually disadvantageous for rent-to-rent margins compared with IRPEF on the net (art. 71 c.2 TUIR allows deduction of "spese specificamente inerenti" for redditi diversi — this TUIR rule is from general knowledge, not verified in a fetched source this session).
- The owner continues to pay IRPEF/cedolare on the head lease rent (reddito fondiario) — rent-to-rent therefore implies two layers of taxation on the same unit.
- Comodato breve: comodato is essentially gratuitous (art. 1803 c.c.); a comodatario renting out to third parties needs the comodante's consent (art. 1804 c.2 c.c.; if violated the comodante can demand immediate return plus damages, art. 1804 c.3 — article text known but page not opened in this session: [Brocardi art. 1804](https://www.brocardi.it/codice-civile/libro-quarto/titolo-iii/capo-xiv/art1804.html)). A "modale" comodato with a small charge stays comodato only if the charge is not a real consideration (Cass. 485/2003; Cass. 3021/2001) — [FIMAA/Figini, PDF p. 24](https://fimaamilano.it/public/filetiny/Formazione/figini-sublocazione_locazionediporzionediimmobile_altricontratti_dirittinov.2022.pdf). A disguised rent paid by a "comodatario" would turn the deal into a (sub)lease.

- No AdE circular specifically interpreting how L. 199/2025 c.17 counts units held via sublocazione/comodato (owner vs sublocatore; whether one owner's apartments sublet by two operators count for each). Only secondary commentary found.
- Text of risposta AdE n. 379/2022 not retrieved; no AdE interpello specifically on rent-to-rent located.
- The precise Normattiva text of art. 1 c.595 L. 178/2020 as amended by L. 199/2025 c.17 was not retrieved (only the AdE paraphrase).

## 4. Rent to rent / "affitto per subaffittare": when it is entrepreneurial, partita IVA, CIN, insurance, consent, destinazione d'uso, case law

### Takeaway
Rent to rent is lawful only with the owner's express written consent to total sublet (art. 2 L. 392/78) and, ideally, explicit authorisation for tourist/short-term use; without it the operator faces termination (art. 1455/1456), eviction binding all guests (art. 1595 c.3) and damages. Fiscally, a private individual can use the art. 4 DL 50/2017 regime for at most 2 apartments (2026); from the 3rd apartment, or whenever the activity is organised in business form (or run by a company), it is business income with partita IVA, and the DL 50/2017 regime (incl. cedolare) does not apply. No Cassazione ruling squarely on rent-to-rent, or one awarding the owner the sublessor's profits, was found.

### Cited Findings
- Circ. 24/E/2017 §1: the regime is excluded for short lets within business activity as defined by art. 55 c.2 TUIR and art. 4 DPR 633/72 (services not in art. 2195 c.c. are business if "derivanti dall'esercizio di attività organizzata in forma d'impresa"); also excluded for non-habitual commercial activity (art. 67 c.1 lett. i TUIR). — [Circolare 24/E/2017, PDF p. 5–6](https://ragusa.ance.it/wp-content/uploads/archivet/30233-Circolare%2024-E%20del%2012%20ottobre%202017.pdf)
- From 2026: more than two apartments → presumption of business "da chiunque esercitata" (L. 199/2025 art. 1 c.17). — [AdE guide April 2026, PDF p. 3, 5](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/locazioni_brevi_disciplina_fiscale_e_regole_per_intermediari_aprile-2026)
- From the 3rd property partita IVA is mandatory; a person may need partita IVA even before the threshold if the activity is organised and continuous (commercial/secondary sources). — [Fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/affitti-brevi-restrizioni/); [Sole 24 Ore NT+](https://ntplusfisco.ilsole24ore.com/art/affitti-brevi-cedolare-26percento-e-attivita-d-impresa-ora-tre-alloggi-come-gestire-partita-iva-AIFRCRcB)
- Rent to rent described as a lawful sublet model for profit; key risks: rent owed to owner even when unit is empty; breach of head lease if sublet not authorised. — [Fiscomania, rent to rent](https://fiscomania.com/rent-to-rent/); [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/info-utili/rent-to-rent-immobiliare-vantaggi/993125/); [Immobiliare.it](https://www.immobiliare.it/news/economia/investimenti-immobiliari/che-cose-il-rent-to-rent-e-come-funziona-407141/)
- CIN (art. 13-ter DL 145/2023, conv. L. 191/2023) is required for units used for tourist/short lets and tourist accommodation; issued via BDSR (Ministero del Turismo); must be displayed outside the building and in every listing; sanctions reported €500–8,000 depending on size (secondary). — [Quotidiano Più](https://www.quotidianopiu.it/dettaglio/10793379/locazioni-brevi-liscrizione-nella-banca-dati-nazionale); [Altroconsumo](https://www.altroconsumo.it/casa-energia/casa-condominio/speciali/codice-cin); [TeamSystem](https://www.teamsystem.com/magazine/horeca/cin-affitti-brevi-strutture-ricettive-esposizione-annunci-sanzioni/)
- Landlord's cedolare on the head lease where the tenant is a business: Cass. Sez. ### Inferences
- The "Cass. SU 11 novembre 2019 n. 29105" mentioned in the brief could NOT be confirmed as a ruling on restitution of sublet profits — do not cite it without verification on italgiure.
- Recommended head-lease clauses for rent to rent (inferred from the rules above): express consent to total sublet (art. 2 L. 392/78) and to sublocazione breve/turistica via portals; obligation of the operator to obtain CIN/regional code (CIR), SCIA where regional law requires, Alloggiati Web, tourist tax; insurance (RC verso terzi/ospiti, danni all'immobile); indemnity for condominium claims; owner's acknowledgement that cedolare on the head lease is still available (Cass. 12395/2024) or price adjusted for the AdE risk; express 1456 clause for the owner; regulation of what happens to bookings on termination (art. 1595 c.3).
- Choice of head-lease type for an operator-company: a 4+4 residential lease to a company is lawful; L. 431/98 tourist-exclusion means the head lease is a normal residential lease while the sub-contracts are tourist lets outside L. 431/98.

## 5. Sublet in canone concordato and cedolare secca contracts

### Takeaway
The ministerial model contract for canone concordato (DM 16/01/2017, Allegato A, compulsory model under art. 2 c.3 L. 431/98) reportedly bans sublet "salvo espresso patto scritto contrario", so sublet requires written consent. The tenant cannot "lose" the cedolare because cedolare is the landlord's option on the head lease; the tenant-sublocatore can use cedolare only on his own sublets ≤30 days (art. 4 c.3 DL 50/2017). Sublets >30 days are taxed at ordinary IRPEF as redditi diversi (art. 67 c.1 lett. h TUIR), cedolare not available.

### Cited Findings
- Model wording quoted: "L'immobile deve essere destinato esclusivamente a civile abitazione del conduttore..." and "Salvo espresso patto scritto contrario, è fatto divieto di sublocazione" (user quote on forum; check official Allegato A). — [CondominioWeb forum](https://www.condominioweb.com/forum/quesito/contratto-di-affitto-concordato-che-limiti-ci-sono-197326/)
- DM 16 gennaio 2017 (GU n. 62 del 15/3/2017): general criteria for local agreements for canone concordato (art. 2 c.3 L. 431/98), transitori and studenti universitari (art. 5 c.1–3); art. 3 covers student contracts. — [DM 16/01/2017 (copy, Comune di Orbassano)](https://orbassano-api.municipiumapp.it/s3/4772/allegati/dm-16-gennaio-2017.pdf)
- Cedolare unavailable for sublocazioni (redditi diversi, art. 67 c.1 lett. h TUIR) — circ. 26/E/2011, superseded only for short lets by DL 50/2017. — [Fiscal Focus](https://www.fiscal-focus.it/quotidiano/altre-tematiche/notizie-free/modello-redditi-locazione-di-breve-durata-cedolare-secca-e-sublocazioni,3,95637); [AdE guide April 2026, PDF p. 6](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/locazioni_brevi_disciplina_fiscale_e_regole_per_intermediari_aprile-2026)
- Landlord's cedolare on a head lease to a business tenant: allowed by Cass. 12395/2024 (see §4), AdE contrary. — [Cass. 12395/2024](https://lentepubblica.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Cassazione-Sentenza-12395-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- A tenant on canone concordato who sublets the whole flat at market or tourist rates with consent is lawful; but the head landlord's concordato benefits (cedolare 10%, IMU reductions) rest on the head lease, and nothing found suggests they are lost by an authorised sublet. An unauthorised tourist sublet is a breach of the model's "civile abitazione del conduttore" clause, giving termination grounds.
- Whether a sublet of a concordato unit must itself respect the concordato rent bands: no source found; sublet rent is "completamente libero" under general rules (Figini PDF p. 12).

### Gaps
- Official Allegato A text not retrieved (the downloaded DM copy contains only the decree articles, not the annexes).
- No source on whether the landlord loses concordato tax benefits if the tenant sublets.

## 6. Studenti: sublocazione and posti letto

### Takeaway
Student contracts (art. 5 c.2–3 L. 431/98; DM 16/01/2017 art. 3) usually ban sublet in the model, so a student needs written consent to sublet a room; partial sublet with raccomandata is the fallback under art. 2 L. 392/78 only if not contractually banned. Letting individual "posti letto" may require affittacamere-type authorisation under regional rules (secondary claim).

### Cited Findings
- Guides state sublet/assignment is prohibited in student contracts; with owner consent a departing student can be replaced. — [Fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/contratto-di-locazione-per-studenti-universitari-guida); [Wikicasa (snippet)](https://www.wikicasa.it/news/?p=26306)
- Claim: single beds cannot be let without authorisation held by affittacamere (secondary, unverified, likely region-dependent). — [Fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/contratto-locazione-studenti-universitari/)
- Student contracts: model by MIT, duration 6–36 months, registration within 30 days. — [Fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/contratto-di-locazione-per-studenti-universitari-guida)
- Forum views: in transitori/student contracts sublet is allowed if agreed in writing, and a transitory sublet not exceeding the head lease is not prohibited (non-consolidated). — [CondominioWeb forum](https://www.condominioweb.com/forum/quesito/contratto-di-locazione-transitorioalcuni-quesiti-28901/)

### Inferences
- A student subletting a room for >30 days earns redditi diversi taxed at IRPEF (no cedolare); for ≤30 days (e.g., summer Airbnb of a room) cedolare is available if consented.

### Gaps
- No official/jurisprudential source on posti letto sublet found; UDU-CGIL-SUNIA guide (2024) not opened.

## 7. Registrazione del contratto di sublocazione (>30 days)

### Takeaway
Short sublets (≤30 days) need no registration; sublets >30 days must be registered like any lease (within 30 days), paying imposta di registro (2% of annual rent, minimum €67, per general registry rules) and bollo, with no cedolare option; the income is redditi diversi under ordinary IRPEF.

### Cited Findings
- "per i contratti di locazione di durata non superiore ai 30 giorni non vi è l'obbligo di registrazione"; cedolare replaces registro and bollo where a short contract is registered. — [AdE guide April 2026, PDF p. 6, 8](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/locazioni_brevi_disciplina_fiscale_e_regole_per_intermediari_aprile-2026)
- The 30-day exemption from registration is computed on all contracts with the same tenant in the year ("trenta giorni complessivi nell'anno"), whereas the DL 50/2017 30-day limit refers to the single contract. — [Confedilizia Nota 2017, PDF p. 1–2](https://www.confedilizia.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/Nota-locazioni-brevi.pdf)
- Before DL 50/2017, cedolare could not apply to sublets (redditi diversi). — [Fiscal Focus](https://www.fiscal-focus.it/quotidiano/altre-tematiche/notizie-free/modello-redditi-locazione-di-breve-durata-cedolare-secca-e-sublocazioni,3,95637)

### Inferences
- Since cedolare is excluded for sublets >30 days, the registro 2% (art. 5 Tariffa parte I DPR 131/1986) applies — this rate/minimum comes from general knowledge, not a document fetched this session.

### Gaps
- No AdE document fetched explicitly confirming the 2% registro rate for sublocazioni; verify on the AdE "registrazione contratti di locazione" page.
